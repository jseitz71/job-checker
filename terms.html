What Job Checker is

Job Checker is a free screening aid. It compares a job posting you provide against common patterns seen in ghost jobs and job scams, and gives an estimated rating from 1 to 5.

Ratings are estimates, not facts

  • A rating is an automated estimate based on the text you paste and the answers you give. It is not a finding that any job is fake, that any company acted dishonestly, or that a job is safe.
  • Companies don't publish which postings lead to hires, so no tool can know for certain. Verify a job directly with the employer before relying on it.
  • Job Checker is not legal, financial or career advice.

Looked-up suggestions can be wrong

Automatic lookups summarize public search results. Search results can be out of date, incomplete, or about a different company with a similar name. Each suggestion shows its sources; check them before you rely on it. A suggestion only counts toward the rating after you confirm it.

Use it fairly

  • Use Job Checker for your own job search.
  • Don't present a rating as a statement of fact about a company or person, or use it to harass, defame or mislead anyone.
  • Paste only content you're allowed to share with yourself, such as a page you're viewing or a message sent to you.
