Job Checker

Is this job actually being filled? Paste a LinkedIn job page and get a 1–5 rating, with every finding traced to its source.

Try an example:
Step 1 of 4

Paste the job page

  1. Open the job on its own page: click the job title so the address looks like linkedin.com/jobs/view/…
  2. If the description ends with “… see more”, click it so the full text shows.
  3. Press Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C (⌘+A, ⌘+C on a Mac).
  4. Click Paste & check, or click the box below and paste.

Copy the whole page. Menus and sidebars are fine; Job Checker ignores them. You can also paste just a job description or a recruiter's message.

Your paste is read in your browser and isn't stored. Job Checker never visits LinkedIn itself.

Step 2 of 4

Review what we found

Check each detail against the job post. Each one shows the line it came from. To fix one, click its pencil, make the change, then click Save. Saved changes are marked Edited and count toward your rating.

How this rating was worked out

Every posting starts at 30 points, the middle of “Uncertain”. Warning signs add points and good signs subtract them. The total picks the rating; a scam deal-breaker sets it to 1.

A screening aid based on common patterns, not proof either way. Companies don't publish which postings lead to hires, so even a 5 is worth confirming on the company's own careers page.
